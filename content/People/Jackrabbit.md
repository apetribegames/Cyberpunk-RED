A [[Pack Rats]] Netrunner. Jackrabbit is a Black man in his early 40s with a short-cropped beard and extensive Neuralware.

In [[Session 2]], [[The Crew|the Crew]] found him in a coma in the [[Pack Rats]] compound with the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] slotted into his Neural Link. [[Hazard]] managed to carefully extract the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] without killing him, and [[The Crew|the Crew]] then extracted him from the compound 

In [[Session 3]], [[John Camden - Patriot|John]] administered medical care to him and questioned him about the [[memory chip]]. He was appreciative, cooperative, and set up a meeting between [[the Crew]] and [[Ruslan Sheripov]].
