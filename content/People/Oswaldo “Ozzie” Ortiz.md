A fixer operating in Night City. Ozzie has a reputation for being decent at his job. He's not a big-shot, but he's reliable and has connections in Night City. He's a short Latino man in his mid-30s. He's bald and wiry. He has obvious neural Cyberware in his head and a pair of plastic Cyberlegs. He's often found at [[Smoke & Mirrors]].
