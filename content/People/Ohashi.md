[[The Crew]]’s mysterious new employer. Ohashi is a chain-smoking Japanese man in his early-60s. He's bald, wears an old, wrinkled suit, and small circular sunglasses.

In [[Session 1]], Ohashi hired [[the Crew]] to retrieve a [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] from a [[Pack Rats|Pack Rat]] compound using a [[Ohashi's Tracker|tracker]] that he provided. He was cagey when asked questions, but offered 1,000eb for person for the job.
