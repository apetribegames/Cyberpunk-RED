The gregarious leader of a scavenger group colloquially known as the [[Sewer Soviets]]. He's a poorly shaved, overweight, Chechnyan man. Scars crisscross his face and his neck is made entirely out of metal. He seems to always be wearing a radiation suit (minus the helmet). 

He can sometimes be found at [[Rusted Jade]].

In [[Session 3]], he hired [[the Crew]] to hijack a truck from the [[Iron Skulls]] in exchange for information about the origins of the [[memory chip]].
