**Summary**
After questioning and releasing the two [[Pack Rats|Pack Rat]] members that they'd knocked out in [[Session 2]], [[The Crew]] brought [[Jackrabbit]] to a medical facility where [[John Camden - Patriot|John]] administered care to him using a cryotank. [[The Crew]] then split up for the night to rest.

The next day, [[Hazard]] managed to decompile the [[memory chip]] and learned that sophisticated code had been injected into to make it act as a key. If the [[memory chip]] is slotted into an "approved" Neural Link, the wearer will be able to open a specific door using their Interface Plugs. However, if the [[memory chip]] is slotted into an "unapproved" Neural Link, it will immediately deploy countermeasures and overload the wearer's brain with a neural spike, causing them to fall into a coma. Every few minutes thereafter, it'll send more spikes to torture them. It will also ping its location over a specific frequency (which is how [[The Crew]] found it in the first place). [[Hazard]] was unable to remove to remove the countermeasures or duplicate the [[memory chip]].

[[Jackrabbit]] woke up from his coma and [[John Camden - Patriot|John]] continued to administer medical care to him while coaxing him for answers. [[Jackrabbit]] (like the rest of the [[Pack Rats]]) seemed completely unaware of what the [[memory chip]] was or who the group that attacked their compound were. He confirmed that the [[Pack Rats]] purchased the [[memory chip]] from [[Ruslan Sheripov]] and his group of so-called "[[Sewer Soviets]]." [[The Crew]] allowed [[Jackrabbit]] to leave and return to his people.

[[The Crew]] then turned the [[memory chip]] and [[Ohashi's Tracker|tracker]] over to [[Ohashi]] and received their payment. [[Ohashi]], in typical fashion, offered few answers when asked. He also seemed genuinely unaware of who the Oni-mask wearing group were. He tasked [[the Crew]] with finding out where the [[memory chip]] came from originally. [[The Crew]] accepted.

[[Jackrabbit]] set up a meeting with [[the Crew]] and [[Ruslan Sheripov|Ruslan]] at a scavenger bar called [[Rusted Jade]] (which [[Leonard 'Leo' Hughes - Bullpen|Bullpen]] had previously staked out). [[Ruslan Sheripov|Ruslan]] offered [[the Crew]] the information they sought in exchange for hijacking a cargo truck from a neo-Nazi gang called the [[Iron Skulls]]. [[The Crew]] accepted after haggling for 25% of the cut from the job.

Thrilling heroics ensued. Katanas and rocket launchers were employed. Many [[Iron Skulls]] were killed. 

[[The Crew]] then successfully delivered the truck to the [[Sewer Soviets]], and [[Ruslan Sheripov]] told them that a few weeks ago, the [[Sewer Soviets]] had gone on a salvage mission deeper into the Hot Zone than they usually go. There, they stumbled upon a man walking by himself through the ruins who immediately opened fire on them. They killed him and stripped him for his gear and cyberware. He was wearing an Oni mask, and had both a Braindance recorder and viewer, which [[Ruslan Sheripov]] turned over to [[the Crew]].

[[The Crew]] watched the Braindance scenes that the man had recorded. 

The first scene showed him jacking into the Old Net using an abandoned access point (presumably somewhere in the Hot Zone), before getting pursued by rabid AI constructs. He was rescued by a woman made entirely out of glowing blue points of light wearing a kimono.

The second scene (also in the Old Net) showed him kneeling in a circle with a handful of other people, centered around the woman from before. She walked up to each kneeling individual, handed them a glowing golden sphere, gently touched their foreheads, and an Oni mask appeared over their faces.

The third scene showed the man walking through an underground facility in the real world, alongside to other people wearing makeshift Oni masks. The facility was old, covered in dust, and empty besides for the three people in the scene. The man eventually reached a large metal door with the words "Arasaka Covert Intelligence Laboratory. Authorized Personnel Only." written on it. He man jacked his Interface Plugs into a keypad and the door slowly slid open.

The Braindance ended before [[the Crew]] could see what was behind the door.
