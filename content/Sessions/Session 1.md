# Session 1

**Summary**
[[The Crew]] each received a message from Ozzie, a fixer they’re acquainted with, to meet at [[Smoke & Mirrors]] for a job. [[Oswaldo “Ozzie” Ortiz|Ozzie]] introduced [[the Crew]] to each other before taking them to a back room and introducing them to [[Ohashi]], their new employer. [[Ohashi]] asked the party to retrieve a [[memory chip]] from a compound controlled by the [[Pack Rats]], using whatever means they see fit. [[Ohashi]] was cagey when asked about the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]], but provided a [[Ohashi's Tracker|tracker]] to aid [[the Crew]] in its retrieval, and warned that the [[Pack Rats]] compound might be protected by turret emplacements and Net countermeasures. He offered to pay 1,000eb per person for the job. [[The Crew]] accepted the job.

**Player Notes**
- [[Ignacio “Nacho” Alvarez - Vulcan|Nacho]] inspected the [[Ohashi's Tracker|tracker]] and deduced that it was built to track objects broadcasting over a specific frequency. It looks like it could be pre-war technology.
- [[Cai Bowen - Nine-Tenth's|Cai]] narrowly lost an arm wrestling contest to [[Leonard “Leo” Hughes - Bullpen|Bullpen]] and received an amateurish tattoo of nine-tenth’s of a pie slice as part of a bet.
