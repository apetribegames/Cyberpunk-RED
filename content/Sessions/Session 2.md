**Summary**
[[The Crew]] successfully infiltrated the [[Pack Rats|Pack Rat]] compound using a combination of Netrunning and stealth. [[Ignacio 'Nacho' Alvarez - Vulcan|Nacho]] managed to disable most of the compound's electronic countermeasures, allowing the remainder of [[The Crew|the Crew]] to break in undetected through the back door and knock out two [[Pack Rats|Pack Rat]] members. Using the [[Ohashi's Tracker|tracker]] given to them by [[Ohashi]] to track the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]], [[The Crew|the Crew]] managed to track its location to a security room in the compound. Upon entering the room, [[The Crew|the Crew]] discovered [[Jackrabbit]], a comatose Netrunner with the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] slotted into his Neural Link. They also found journal entries explaining the events that led to [[Jackrabbit]] being in this state, as detailed below:

- Eight days prior, the [[Pack Rats]] bought a Neural Link from a group of Neo-Soviet scavengers operating in the Hot Zone. They discovered the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] hidden in the Neural Link.
- [[Jackrabbit]] slotted the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] into his Neural Link to see what was on it. He immediately suffered a seizure and fell into a coma.
- Before attempting to remove the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]], the [[Pack Rats]] plugged it into a computer and managed to decompile some of its code. They discovered that any attempt to remove the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] without disabling it first would kill [[Jackrabbit]]. The [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] seemed to be actively torturing [[Jackrabbit]].
- The [[Pack Rats]] had been unsuccessfully trying to disable the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] since then.
- Three days ago, the [[Pack Rats]] were contacted by an anonymous buyer (presumably [[Ohashi]]) interested in purchasing any recently acquired Neural Cyberware. They deduced that this wasn't a coincidence and that the buyer knew about the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] and potentially what was contained on it. The [[Pack Rats]] were unwilling to remove the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] if it meant killing [[Jackrabbit]], so they declined a sale.
- Since the buyer knew that the [[Pack Rats]] had the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]], the [[Pack Rats]] are worried that the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] itself is somehow pinging its location ([[The Crew|the Crew]] know this to be true, since [[Ohashi]] gave them a [[Ohashi's Tracker|tracker]] for it). The [[Pack Rats]] were planning on packing up their operations at their compound and moving underground in the next few days just to be safe.

[[Hazard]] managed to carefully extract the [[Memory Chip|memory chip]] from [[Jackrabbit]]'s Neural Link without killing him. While discussing what to do next, the compound was assaulted by an unknown group wearing makeshift oni masks. [[The Crew]] decided to escape, taking [[Jackrabbit]] and the two [[Pack Rats|Pack Rat]] members they knocked out with them. A firefight on the way out revealed that the members of the unknown group were wired to a dead man's switch that detonated their heads upon death. [[The Crew]] successfully escaped with no casualties.
