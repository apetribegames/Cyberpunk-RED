There are multiple journal entries for this past week.
- 8 days ago, the Pack Rats bought a Neural Link from a group of Neo-Soviet Scavengers (led by Ruslan Sheripov) operating in the Hot Zone. They discovered a Memory Chip hidden in the Neural Link.
- The Pack Rat Netrunner, a man named Jackrabbit (presumably the person unconscious on the cot), slotted the Memory Chip into his Neural Link to see what was on it. He immediately suffered a seizure and fell into a coma.
- Before attempting to remove the Memory Chip, the Pack Rats plugged it into a computer and managed to decompile some of its code. They discovered that any attempt to remove the Memory Chip without disabling it first would kill Jackrabbit. The Memory Chip seems to be actively torturing Jackrabbit, but they're not sure how.
- The Pack Rats have been trying to disable the Memory Chip since then but have been unsuccessful. They have no idea what's on it.
- 3 days ago, the Pack Rats were contacted by an anonymous buyer interested in purchasing any recently acquired Neural Cyberware. They deduced that this wasn't a coincidence and that the buyer knew about the Memory Chip and potentially what was contained on it. The Pack Rats were unwilling to remove the Memory Chip if it meant killing Jackrabbit, so they declined a sale. They tried both to extract information from the buyer and to track them down, but the buyer got suspicious and ghosted them. You deduce the buyer was probably Ohashi.
- Since the buyer knew that the Pack Rats had the Memory Chip, the Pack Rats are worried that the Memory Chip itself is somehow pinging its location (you know this to be true, since Ohashi gave you a tracker for it). They're planning on packing up their operations at this compound and moving underground in the next few days just to be safe.

[[The Crew]] decided to escape, taking [[Jackrabbit]] and the two [[Pack Rats|Pack Rat]] members they knocked out with them. A firefight on the way out revealed that the members of the unknown group were wired to a dead man's switch that detonated their heads upon death. [[The Crew]] successfully escaped with no casualties.
